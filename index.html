Thrilling Of Tremors

This was made when I was in high school. It recreates the famous painting "The Creation of Adam" using characters from the asymmetrical horror game Dead by Daylight. It utilizes the character Ghost Face, and the Entity, the chaotic god that rules within the game. The title "Thrilling of Tremors" is a reference to Ghost Face's perk, Thrilling Tremors, which is a strong and versatile ability that prevents the survivors from making progress. I enjoyed Ghost Face as a character, since his gameplay was very unique from the rest of the cast, so I created this piece to signify that Ghost Face is a fun and intersting character, even if he isn't the strongest.
